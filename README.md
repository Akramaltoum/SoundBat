# SoundBat

A neon soundboard for Discord. Click a pad or hit a hotkey mid-game and your bat plays the sound for everyone in your voice channel.

## Download for Windows 10/11

**[Download SoundBat.zip](https://github.com/Akramaltoum/SoundBat/releases/latest/download/SoundBat.zip)**

Right-click SoundBat.zip and pick Extract All. Open the SoundBat folder and double-click SoundBat.exe. If Windows says "protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. The setup screens take about 3 minutes.

Your bot, account and sounds stay on your own PC.
