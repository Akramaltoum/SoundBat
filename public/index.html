SOUNDBAT

Play any sound straight into your Discord call — from the app, your phone, or a hotkey mid-game. Let's get your bat flying.

4 quick steps · about 3 minutes

1 · Make your SoundBat account

This is you inside SoundBat. It stays on this PC — no email or password.

Pick your bat — or upload a photo
Your glow

2 · Give your bat a body

SoundBat plays sounds through your own Discord bot. Making one is free — Discord just needs you to click a few buttons.

1Click Open Discord below and log in if it asks.
2Click New Application, name it SoundBat, tick the box, Create.
3Click Bot on the left → Reset Token → Yes, do it! → Copy.
4Come back to SoundBat — it grabs the token by itself.
Open Discord ↗

3 · Let it into your server

Add your bat to the Discord server you hang out in. Pick the server, then Authorize.

Waiting for it to join…

4 · Which one is you?

Hop into any voice channel in that server — then click your name so the bat knows who to follow.

Looking for people in voice channels…
